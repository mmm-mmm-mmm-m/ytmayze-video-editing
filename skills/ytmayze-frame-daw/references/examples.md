# Framing examples

These examples explain decisions carried forward from the source edits. Geometry must come from the current footage.

- A tight piano-roll crop makes note entry readable, but loses the last click at the right edge. Widen or pan through the release; inspect the whole gesture.
- Adjacent clips continue one action but reset their crop positions. Match the outgoing and incoming framing state to remove the bob.
- An idle velocity lane can be hidden. When velocity is the actual demonstration, show it and move the webcam if necessary.
- Keep portrait and landscape layouts as separately named variants. Preserve the useful earlier version when changing geometry.

A framing pass does not authorize changing note timing, music gain or picture cuts.
