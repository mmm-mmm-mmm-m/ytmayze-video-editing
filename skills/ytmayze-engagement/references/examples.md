# Engagement examples

- A quiet reaction may work with a stable hold or slow zoom. Aggressive wiggle can call attention to awkward movement without improving the joke.
- A reference noun in the narration does not by itself justify a literal overlay. Give the visual a setup, recognizable payoff and clear return.
- Centering/scaling a reaction word should preserve its font, proportions, timing and effects unless the request includes those changes.
- Large stretched full-frame words may suit a chosen lyric treatment. Ordinary process captions should follow the project’s established caption language.
- A CTA needs current supplied offer text. An old offer from a reference video is not evidence of a current product term.

Use only media supplied for the task or obtained with suitable permission; do not distribute source meme assets as part of the skill.
