# Vertical Plugin Demos

## Pick the baseline and the proof

Start from the strongest approved cut or reference passage. For each revision, write down what to borrow, what to change, and what to carry forward intact. Name the ranges and source takes. Protect related choices across picture, timing, narration, beat, annotations, and delivery. Make a named editable copy. A request to fix the cursor, for example, changes its path and linked click cues. Compare the gesture against the surrounding pace and mix.

Start with the promise a viewer can understand from the first image or a short line. Think through the sequence with sound off. Let the viewer see the problem, the product's result, a readable action that connects the two, and the next step. Choose how much proof belongs in the hook. A clear still can introduce the result and save the animation or sound for Play. An early working preview can earn attention when it makes an unfamiliar promise clear. Test the actual footage and opening line before choosing.

Use native product captures for the action and result. Edit out waiting and secondary settings if the core action still reads. Give the viewer time to see a click or drag complete. Enlarge the control or result while honoring requests to show the whole plugin. Use one cue to lead the eye to the actual target. Confirm it tracks through pans and zooms. If the edit uses a mock interaction, make sure the approved claim is backed by the real product behavior.

## Give speech and music separate moments

Use the separate voice and beat tracks. Find the frames where the viewer should listen to the explanation and where the beat or plugin output should take over. Try a quieter or filtered bed underneath the hook. Compare a gentle level change with a more audible filter on the same voice take and picture. Restore the beat's weight and presence at a meaningful visual or musical cue, such as a larger result shot, and give the demonstration space before the next line.

Keep source time continuous when a held screen sits over a playing beat. Match the restored picture to the musical position at Play. An intentional audio cut belongs on a motivated phrase boundary and needs a listening pass. Sync mouse-down, drag, release, and clicks to the visible actions. Apply more detailed foley guidance from `$ytmayze-design-sound` when needed.

## Finish for the feed

Compose the export in the intended aspect ratio, then check physical cropping and interface coverage as separate problems. Protect the visual subject, legible controls, text, and CTA in the full range of motion. Tighten the source framing around the main subject before enlarging the whole card into a risky gutter. Choose a CTA that reads on mute and conveys the intended action. Test the last-to-first join if the edit is meant to loop.

Compare changes in context with the baseline on sound-on and muted playback. For gutter loss, unstable crops, HDR, or trial performance, use [Review and delivery](references/review-and-delivery.md). That check distinguishes the encoded file from the presentation in the platform app. Use the same workflow to turn a narrated markup recording into timestamped edit notes.
